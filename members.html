卢锦胜Jinsheng Lu

Email: jinslu@whu.edu.cn Email: jinslu@whu.edu.cn

Google Scholar ORCID ResearchGate

武汉大学动力与机械学院教授，博士生导师，国家高层次海外青年人才项目入选者。本科和博士毕业于浙江大学光电科学与工程学院；哈佛大学博士后。主要从事集成光学领域的光驱动系统和芯片研究，涵盖激光物理中光与物质相互作用、光学超表面和集成光学芯片等前沿交叉领域。 Jinsheng is a Professor and Doctoral Supervisor at the School of Power and Mechanical Engineering, Wuhan University, and a recipient of the National Overseas High-Level Young Talent Program. He obtained his B.Eng. and Ph.D. degrees from the College of Optical Science and Engineering, Zhejiang University, and completed his postdoctoral training at Harvard University. His research focuses on optically driven systems and photonic chips in integrated optics, encompassing frontier interdisciplinary areas such as light–matter interactions in laser physics, optical metasurfaces, and integrated photonic chips.

已发表20余篇SCI论文，其中一作/通讯（含共同）文章9篇，包含Physical Review Letters（4篇）、Science Advances（2篇）、Optica、Light: Science & Applications等，其他共同作者的文章包括Science等。受邀担任Nature旗下npj Nanophotonics期刊编委会成员、北大中文核心期刊《光通信研究》青年编委、2025年亚洲光电子会议（SPIE Photonics Asia）组委会委员，长期担任Adv. Opt. Photonics、Light Sci. Appl.、Opt. Lett.、Opt. Express、Photonics Res.等20多个国际知名期刊审稿人，受邀审稿100余次。 He has published more than 20 SCI-indexed papers, including 9 first/corresponding-author papers. His representative first-author publications have appeared in Physical Review Letters (4), Science Advances (2), Optica, and Light: Science & Applications. He has also co-authored papers published in leading journals such as Science.

He has been invited to serve as an Editorial Board Member of npj Nanophotonics (Nature Portfolio), a Young Editorial Board Member of Optical Communication Research, and a member of the Organizing Committee of SPIE Photonics Asia 2025. He has also served as a reviewer for more than 20 internationally renowned journals, including Advanced Optical Materials, Advanced Photonics, Light: Science & Applications, Optics Letters, Optics Express, and Photonics Research, and has been invited to review more than 100 manuscripts.
